# MC Кейк — visual and interaction system

## Direction
A cherry pâtisserie with buttery cream, dusty rose and burgundy glaze. Large Cormorant Garamond Cyrillic headings carry the editorial composition; Manrope makes the copy and controls readable. The hero and gallery offer generous cake silhouettes, with text in separate space. The layer story “Большие чувства. Слой за слоем.” remains the central visual narrative.

The main action is to find a visual direction, freely choose color, shape and details, then discuss the idea with the real business by phone. The form is an explicitly disclosed browser-only demonstration. No business hours, real prices, production guarantees, stock, delivery promises or customer reviews are invented.

## Motion is code
All cakes, including all three inspiration cards, are generated by `cakeSVG()` from cylinders, elliptical piping, ridges, drapes, cherries, bow paths, candles and plate geometry. The layer story is another hand-authored SVG composition. No raster images, video, image frames or animated textures are used.

System `prefers-reduced-motion` is the only motion setting. A change reverts GSAP contexts, clears interaction tweens/listeners and restores a complete static composition. There is no manual toggle and no stored preference. Normal native scrolling stays available. No perpetual automatic animation is used: the repeating ribbon consists of two identical tracks and follows native scroll without exposing an end.

| Area | Interaction | Stable controls and alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header | Inner Contacts face follows pointer by at most 5 px | Anchor hitbox does not transform; disabled for coarse input/reduced motion; focus resets face |
| Hero | SVG cherries, bow, tiers and orbit respond independently | Copy and CTA stable; local cherry cursor only over non-text cake SVG |
| Ribbon | Two identical full-content tracks create a seamless scroll band | Decorative, no auto-play or drag, native scrolling |
| Introduction | Early scroll rotates cherry; bounded pointer depth | Text remains fixed and selectable |
| Gallery | SVG cake art settles on early entry and responds to pointer | Captions and preset buttons outside animated geometry; three code-drawn cakes |

| Contacts | Decorative SVG bow follows scroll and pointer | Fields, form, phone and map remain fixed |

## Constructor and form
The three constructor tabs use a roving tab stop with explicit tab/tabpanel relationships. Selecting any tab retains all current choices. “Получить концепт” is always available. The resulting concept can be edited in place; its text updates with the current choices. Reset returns to the original cake. Sending the idea to the form only fills an in-memory textarea.

The occasion field uses a select-only combobox. Arrow/Home/End/typeahead change the active option without committing; Enter/Space/Tab commit. Escape and outside pointer close without changing the value. Tab is not trapped. Placement and maximum height are recalculated on resize/scroll, without closing during focus-induced scrolling. The hidden form value, selected option and visible text reset together.

Form validation checks a name, 10–15 phone digits with an optional single leading plus, a valid occasion, and explicit demo acknowledgement. It focuses the first invalid control. Success resets the form and says no data was sent or stored. There is no fetch, analytics or storage. The submit button starts disabled until the JavaScript handler is installed, so a no-JavaScript page cannot make a GET request with entered personal data.

## Accessibility
Sticky header keeps Contacts visible at all widths. Focus and anchors account for its height. Text selection changes only letter color with a transparent background. SVG service arrows start northeast and become east only on hover/focus. The ordinary cursor remains over text, links and fields; a small local SVG cherry cursor is limited to the non-text cake illustrations on fine pointers. Touch interaction never depends on hover. Focus remains visible on all controls, including dropdown and constructor tabs.
